# IJCAI 2025 Tutorial  
**Multimodal Large Language Models for Visually Rich Document Understanding (VRDU)**

📧 **Primary Contact**: Yihao Ding, University of Sydney ([yihao.ding@sydney.edu.au](mailto:yihao.ding@sydney.edu.au))

---

## 🧠 Overview

The rising demand for a Visually Rich Document (VRD) understanding spans various domains such as business, law, and medicine, aiming to enhance the efficiency of document-related tasks. Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are particularly suited for VRD understanding (VRDU) tasks, as they possess extensive real-world knowledge, enabling zero-shot or few-shot learning. This tutorial will systematically overview LLM/MLLM-driven VRDU frameworks, analyzing current technical trends. It will begin by introducing common document understanding tasks, along with associated benchmarks and techniques. Following this, a summary of existing LLM/MLLM-based VRDU frameworks will cover training strategies, inference methods, and data preparation. To solidify understanding, two hands-on labs will be provided, ensuring participants grasp not only theoretical concepts but also practical applications using state-of-the-art techniques to address real-world challenges.

## 📌 Topics Covered

- **VRDU Tasks**: Layout Analysis, Key Info Extraction, Document VQA
- **Benchmark Datasets**: FUNSD, DocVQA, CORD, Form-NLU
- **Deep Learning Models**: LayoutLM, SelfDoc, UDoc
- **MLLM Frameworks**: DocLLM, mPLUG-DocOwl, HRVDA
- **Techniques**: Frozen LLMs, Chain-of-Thought (CoT), In-context Learning
- **Hands-on Labs**: Pretrained and MLLM-based VRDU with Form-NLU dataset

## 🎯 Target Audience & Welcome

This tutorial is tailored for a broad and diverse audience, including:

- 🧑‍🔬 Researchers  
- 🏢 Industrial developers and practitioners  
- 🤖 Professionals in deep learning, multimodal frameworks, information retrieval, data mining, and document understanding

The focus of the tutorial is on the integration and application of **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)** for intelligent document understanding. It explores how MLLMs enhance document comprehension and retrieval tasks through practical use cases and hands-on labs.

We warmly welcome:

- ✅ Newcomers interested in multimodal learning or MLLMs  
- ✅ Experienced professionals seeking advanced techniques and applications

While a foundational understanding of machine learning and NLP is helpful, it is **not a strict prerequisite**. The tutorial is designed to provide a comprehensive and inclusive learning experience, combining in-depth lectures with interactive, hands-on sessions.
We look forward to your participation and contribution to the discussion!
