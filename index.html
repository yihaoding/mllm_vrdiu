IJCAI 2025 Tutorial

Multimodal Large Language Models for Visually Rich Document Understanding (VRDU)

Primary Contact: Yihao Ding, University of Sydney (yihao.ding@sydney.edu.au)

Overview

This tutorial will explore how Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) enhance Visually Rich Document Understanding (VRDU) across domains such as business, law, and medicine. The session includes both lectures and hands-on labs covering MLLM-based frameworks, document tasks, benchmarks, and real-world applications.

Schedule (3.5 hours)

  • Intro to MLLM for VRDU (20 mins): Motivation & Background
  • VRDU + MLLM Background (60 mins): Tasks, Benchmarks, Lab 1 (Pretrained Frameworks)
  • MLLM for VRDU (85 mins): Frameworks, Training, Inference, Dataset, Lab 2 (MLLM Application)
  • Conclusion (10 mins): Future Trends & Closing

Topics

  • VRDU Tasks: Layout Analysis, Key Info Extraction, DocVQA
  • Benchmark Datasets: FUNSD, DocVQA, CORD, Form-NLU
  • Deep Learning Frameworks: LayoutLM, SelfDoc, UDoc
  • MLLM-based Frameworks: DocLLM, mPLUG-DocOwl, HRVDA
  • Training & Inference Strategies: Frozen LLMs, CoT, In-context Learning
  • Hands-on Labs: Form-NLU with pretrained and MLLM-based models

Target Audience

This tutorial is designed for researchers, developers, and practitioners in deep learning, multimodal learning, NLP, and document intelligence. Both beginners and experts will gain valuable insights and hands-on experience with modern frameworks.

Presenters

  • Feiqi Cao (University of Sydney)
  • Siqu Long (Affiliated, University of Sydney)

  • Yifan Peng (Weill Cornell Medicine)

Why Attend?

Learn how MLLMs like GPT-4o, DocLLM, and others are transforming document understanding with zero/few-shot capabilities and scalable architectures. This tutorial offers an in-depth walkthrough of current challenges, solutions, and emerging trends in VRDU.
